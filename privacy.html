Privacy policy

phone-backup is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: only files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files.
  • What it stores: encrypted backup archives of the owner's own home-automation phone. Nothing is collected from anyone else.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party.
  • Tracking: no analytics, cookies or advertising.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the backup files can be deleted from Google Drive.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
